Randy's public site. Read README.md first.

- Public means public: pushing `main` deploys https://randy.gg. Push or change anything public only on Randy's word. Review happens on `tools/preview.sh` (unlisted, noindexed).
- Work on `main`. The local tag `margin-notes-v1` is the parked margin-notes build, and its history holds a private image (Nov 14 2026: "don't publish the faces study"), so it is never pushed or merged: copy code across from it, never history.
- Dark only (Nov 14 2026: "fully dark mode, I hate light"): no light theme, no device switch.
- The look is Horizon (Nov 16 2026: "horizon is banger, do this"): the header a dawn sky, the posts the land, the type bar on a warm glowing horizon line. Hosted pieces wear it too.
- One page (Nov 16 2026, B picked over the featured card: "no featured post, center the randy thing and then just have the posts underneath"): the link page's header centred (photo, wordmark, the line "me like to make stuff", links), under it the type bar (All, Game, Art, Video, Music) and every post as that card, the first row peeking above the fold as the cue to scroll. No carousel ("kill the switcher"). Every card is the live card ("my fav layout of the card"): image, then one bar with the source's icon, the title, how long ago and no date ("just remove the date and keep the 2 weeks ago"), the arrow. A card goes straight to the post where it lives, in the same tab ("yup normal is best"); a piece hosted here opens fullscreen instead, with a download of the original (Nov 16 2026): art plays its timelapse with no still view ("no need for the piece full screen, just have a download button there for it tho"); a track is "a full experience", the whole screen its cover (its game's logo, else the RANDY wordmark, over a sky seeded per track) and a waveform to scrub. No short descriptions yet ("don't even build out a short desc system yet"). A post with words of his gets its own page (Nov 16 2026: "convert the steam update one into a post, just copy across my content from there, with a link out to the official steam post up top"): his text verbatim, the official post linked on the horizon, his own asides as margin notes after Crafting Interpreters, none invented. Build anything new from that page's own materials.
- None of his older writing on the site for now: it stays in his vault, its archive at that tag until he wants it.
- No packages: `build.mjs` stays one dependency-free file and its output is committed.
- Run `node build.mjs` after any content or build change; screenshot headlessly with `tools/shoot.mjs`, and run `tools/check.mjs` on a served copy (`tools/preview.sh --local` stages one), both under `xvfb-run -a nice -n 10`.
