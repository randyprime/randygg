This is my first time trying to properly paint out skin and this reflective armor, learnt a lot in the process.

It kinda blew my mind that in order to draw something shiny you have to actually think about the entire scene behind you and draw that, not the actual thing itself.

I was also banging my head against the proportions a bunch and had a good reference I could overlay to tell me what I was doing wrong.

Overlaying that and being able to spot what was wrong was huge.

I'm kinda in disbelief staring at how well this turned out in the end honestly.
